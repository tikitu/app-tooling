## Tracking the project

* Plans, progress and open questions live in markdown in this repo, not in
  an issue tracker.

* `PLAN.md` is the index to the documentation. Link every document in
  `docs/` from it; put depth in `docs/`, not in `PLAN.md`.

* Record progress in `PROGRESS.md`, newest first: what was done, what was
  verified, what was learned. Telling a new session "read PLAN.md and
  PROGRESS.md" should be enough to bring it up to speed.

* `PROBLEMS.md` holds open questions, and the reasoning behind decisions
  that would otherwise be lost — the "why we did not do X" that a commit
  message drops.
